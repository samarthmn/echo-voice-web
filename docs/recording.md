---
title: Recording
description: Record online meetings with the extension, in-person conversations with your microphone, or import an existing audio file.
---

Echo has three ways to get audio into your library. Whichever you use, the audio is stored encrypted in your library and transcribed on your Mac.

## Online meetings

Use the [browser extension](extension.md) for Google Meet, Zoom web and Microsoft Teams web.

1. Join the meeting in Brave or Chrome as you normally would.
2. With the meeting tab selected, open the Echo extension popup.
3. Give the recording a title, and choose whether to **Include my microphone** and **Show live text in Echo**.
4. Tick **Participants have consented to recording**, then **Start recording**.
5. **Pause** or **Stop & save** from the popup. Leaving the call, closing the tab or navigating away also ends the recording.

Echo does not join the call as a bot. It captures the tab's audio directly, and your microphone only while you are unmuted. The audio is journaled, encrypted, in the browser and uploaded to Echo when it is reachable, so a recording survives Echo being stopped.

Ask for consent. Recording laws differ between places; many require everyone's agreement.

## In-person meetings

In Echo, choose **New meeting → In person**. Allow microphone access when the browser asks, pick the input device, and record. Keep the Echo tab open while recording. If the browser reports a microphone error, Echo shows what went wrong and lets you start again.

## Uploading a recording

Choose **Upload a recording** on the overview and pick a file. Echo accepts audio files and WebM or MP4 video (it keeps only the audio). Large files upload in chunks and resume if interrupted.

## After recording

Open the meeting and choose **Create transcript**, or turn on **Settings → General → Transcribe after recording** to start automatically. See [transcription models](transcription-models.md) and [speakers](speakers.md).

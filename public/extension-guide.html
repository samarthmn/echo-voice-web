BROWSER EXTENSION

Record your next meeting

Use the meeting you already joined in Brave or Chrome. Keep Echo’s local server running to save recordings to your library.

  1. Protect saved recordings. In extension Setup, create and privately save the recovery phrase, then confirm the requested words. It is separate from the Echo library phrase.
  2. Connect Echo. Open the extension from your browser toolbar. Choose Connect to Echo, then approve the connection on the Echo page that opens.
  3. Choose your meeting service. Enable Google Meet, Zoom Web or Microsoft Teams Web in extension setup. Your browser asks for access only to that service.
  4. Choose your audio. Allow microphone access to include your voice, or choose Record call audio only. Your microphone is excluded whenever meeting controls are muted or cannot be verified.
  5. Start in your meeting tab. Return to the meeting, open Echo in the toolbar, confirm participant consent and press Start. Closing the popup does not stop recording.
  6. Stop and save. Choose Stop & save. Audio stays in the browser until Echo confirms its complete copy. Open the recording in Echo to transcribe it.

Recording works offline. Choose Record without Echo in setup; connect later or export saved audio from Local recordings & recovery. After a browser restart without Echo, enter your extension recovery phrase to unlock older recordings. Optional live text needs an open Echo workspace and an installed Turbo model.

Connection help

Echo unavailable? Start Echo’s local server, then choose Connect to Echo again. If Echo uses another local address, enter it under Advanced in extension setup.

Older Echo version? Update Echo for automatic connection recovery, or create a pairing code in Echo Settings → Browser extension and enter it under Advanced. Approve the request in Echo.

Transfer or export pending audio before uninstalling the extension or deleting your browser profile.

Developer installation

The extension is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. For source builds, run pnpm build:extension, open your browser’s extensions page, enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select extension/dist from the checkout.

See docs/browser-extension.md for development setup and recovery.

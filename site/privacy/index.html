Privacy

Echo records and processes conversations on your computer. You choose when to record and whether to connect optional services.

Prepared October 9, 2026. Publisher: {{publisher}}. Contact: {{supportEmail}}.

This draft describes the current development candidate. Independent security review, release qualification and the hosting configuration must be completed before this becomes a published release policy.

What the recorder handles

After you start a recording, the extension captures audio from the selected meeting tab. If you enable and permit your microphone, it includes your voice only while the meeting is positively identified as active and unmuted. Unknown or stale mute state excludes microphone audio.

The extension also handles the recording title, meeting service and address, timing, call and mute state, consent selection, connection identifiers, local transfer credentials and recording status. Optional live text can appear in its preview. Provider observers read call and mute controls; they do not read chat messages, participant profiles, page transcripts, passwords or session cookies.

The extension never requests or captures camera video. A microphone permission and a consent checkbox do not notify other participants: you must obtain any necessary permission before recording.

Where recordings are saved

The extension saves pending audio and sensitive recording details in encrypted browser storage. Some operational identifiers, recording state and frame counts remain readable for recovery. Its connection credential is encrypted with a device key in the browser profile; this is not a hardware boundary against a stolen, runnable profile.

After pairing and your approval, the extension transfers audio to that Echo library through an authenticated connection on the same computer. Echo's database and audio files use authenticated encryption. The operating system's credential store holds the library key. Authorized Echo can unlock extension history automatically; a separate extension recovery phrase enables offline access after a browser restart.

Encryption protects saved data. Audio and text must be available in memory while playing or processing. An unlocked application or compromised operating-system account can expose them. Exported WAV, transcript and notes files are readable files; library backups use a separate encrypted format.

Network connections and optional services

  • Local transcription: selected speech models are downloaded from their model hosts. Those hosts receive ordinary download requests; local speech recognition processes the recording on your computer.
  • Google Calendar: when you connect it in Echo, read-only authorization lets the app retrieve your calendar events and meeting links. The extension does not receive Google credentials. Calendar is optional.
  • Notes: when you choose ChatGPT, the transcript and notes instructions are sent to OpenAI through the connected account. When you choose local Ollama, those inputs are sent to the configured local Ollama service. These features are optional and have their own service terms.
  • Updates: a qualified app checks its configured update service when you choose Check for updates. Your browser manages store-extension updates separately.

The recording feature introduces no analytics or advertising service. Echo does not upload your recording library to the publisher. The meeting service itself continues to handle the call under its own policies.

Retention, export and deletion

Pending browser audio stays until Echo confirms a complete durable import or you explicitly delete it. After a confirmed import, the extension removes its redundant audio and retains a small receipt linking to Echo. Unsynchronized deletion requires confirmation.

Imported recordings and versions remain in your local library until you delete them. Exported files and backups are separate copies and remain until you remove them. Uninstalling the extension, clearing its storage or deleting its browser profile can erase pending recordings. A recovery phrase unlocks existing data; it cannot recreate deleted data.

You can disconnect the extension or optional accounts in Echo. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not delete recordings already saved in either location. Keep recovery phrases private. The publisher cannot replace a lost phrase or recover data without a usable key.

Support and website data

If you email support, your email address, message and attachments reach {{publisher}} through the email provider. Share only the material needed to explain the problem. Do not send recovery phrases, account tokens, private meeting links or participant recordings.

This site is a local preview and contains no analytics scripts, forms or third-party fonts. Before hosting it, the release policy must identify the hosting provider, request-log handling and retention, plus the support-message retention policy. Those operational choices are still pending.

Limited use

Echo's use of information obtained through Google APIs follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used for the recording, local processing and optional integrations described here, not advertising, sale or credit decisions. The publisher does not receive your local library. Any human review of specific material you send for support requires your explicit consent.

This statement is part of the candidate policy and must be checked against the final package and dashboard declarations before submission.

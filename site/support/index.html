Get help with Echo

Start with the message shown in Echo. Keep any unsynchronized recordings until the problem is resolved.

Contact {{publisher}} at {{supportEmail}}. No response-time guarantee is offered for the preview.

Echo will not connect

Start the local Echo server and open its web address first, then choose Connect to Echo in the extension. Approve the request in Echo and return to the extension. If you use a custom local address, enter it under Advanced. The app and extension must be on the same computer. If the extension says Update Echo, update the local app; audio stays in the browser until a compatible app can receive it safely.

My voice is missing

Check that you chose to include your microphone and allowed microphone access in Setup. Your meeting must also be unmuted. If Echo cannot read its mute state, it excludes your voice and shows a warning. Call-audio-only recording intentionally excludes your microphone.

Saved audio has no transcript

Follow First transcript on Overview. It shows whether the selected speech model is ready and links to model setup or the saved recording. Wait for the model download to finish, then open the meeting and choose Create transcript. Notes and Calendar are optional; recording does not require either.

A recording was interrupted

Open Local recordings & recovery in the extension. Retry transfer with Echo open, or export the saved audio. Keep every exported WAV part and its timeline file. Do not uninstall, reload during capture or clear browser data to troubleshoot missing audio.

Saved recordings are locked

Open the paired Echo app to unlock extension history. If Echo is unavailable after a browser restart, use the extension's recovery phrase. For a locked Echo library, first unlock the operating system's credential store; if its key was lost, follow the repository's storage-and-recovery guide for the private local recovery command. Recovery currently requires command-line assistance. The library and extension phrases are different.

Never send either phrase to support. If all usable keys and the matching phrase are lost, the protected data cannot be decrypted.

Report a problem

Include your Echo and extension versions, macOS or Ubuntu version, browser version, the action you attempted and the exact error text. Say whether a pending recording is still saved in the browser.

Share screenshots only after removing account details, private meeting links and transcript text. Do not attach a library, recording, browser profile, recovery phrase, token or password. Support can first investigate with a synthetic example.

Open a public issue with sanitized details, or use email for a security concern. Public issues are visible to others.

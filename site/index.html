Your browser meeting, your recording

The extension captures call audio and your permitted microphone. It excludes your microphone when the meeting reports it muted or its state cannot be confirmed. Recording starts only when you choose it and confirm participant consent.

Echo never requests camera access. The extension can save recordings while the local Echo server is stopped; the web app and server are needed for transcription and your meeting library.

Recording privacy and limits

Start requires your click and participant consent. This extension captures audio from the chosen meeting tab and an optional microphone. It never uses your camera, reads browser account credentials, joins meetings automatically, or sends audio to a cloud speech service.

Saved audio, meeting details and the Echo connection are encrypted with a key that never leaves this browser. This does not protect an unlocked browser or a stolen browser profile from someone who can run that browser. Exported WAV and timeline files are readable files; save them somewhere private.

Limits: eight hours per recording, 2 GiB of pending audio, five seconds of saving backlog. Audio that has not been confirmed in Echo is never automatically deleted.
